== Verification

cocotb covers the loopback plus UDP, IP, ARP, and MAC gates. The latency source is `sim/results/latency_summary.json`.

The payload loopback budget is at most 20 cycles. The measured echo is 2 cycles, 20 ns at 100 MHz.
